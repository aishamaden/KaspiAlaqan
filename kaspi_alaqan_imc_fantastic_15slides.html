01 · BRAND
What is Kaspi Alaqan?
Product truth, innovation context & launch facts
Product essence
Pay-by-palm — pay with your hand, no card, no phone, no internet connection required. First of its kind in Central Asia.
Security & certification
Certified by Kazakhstan's Agency for Financial Market Regulation. Independent lab tests covering network security, integrity, availability and confidentiality.
Rollout plan
December 2025 — Almaty. 2026 — nationwide. Terminals free for merchants. 0% commission for first 3 months, then Kaspi Pay standard rate.
Global context
Technology proven in Japan 10+ years. Amazon One (USA), Alipay Palm (China). Kazakhstan is the first in the entire CIS region to launch at scale.
Strategic importance
Aligned with Kazakhstan's national goal of a $450B GDP by 2029. Digital technologies like Alaqan are central to reaching this goal per government roadmap.
02 · ARCHETYPE
Brand archetype & tone of voice
Foundation for consistent integration across every channel
Primary archetype
THE INNOVATOR
Secondary: The Everyman — democratizing frontier tech for every Kazakhstani

Brand voice

Confident, plain-spoken, inspiring. Zero tech jargon. Like a smart friend, not a corporate announcement.

Tone

Modern, warm, bold. Kazakhstani spirit at global standards. Proud but never arrogant.

Core values

Freedom. Security. Simplicity. Kazakhstani pride. The future — right now, not someday.

Key association to build across all integrations "Your palm is your new wallet." Alaqan = freedom from all physical constraints. We don't make payments more convenient — we eliminate the barrier between a person and their money entirely.
How to reach TOM (Top of Mind) Repeat the open-palm gesture in every single channel. One unified visual symbol — the open hand — from OOH billboards to TikTok clips. Frequency target: 7+ brand touchpoints per month with core TA to lock in recall.

04 · BEHAVIORAL PROFILE

User scenario · habits · behavioral patterns · Alaqan touchpoints

06 · COMPETITORS
Competitor analysis & positioning
Communication tone review · what to mirror and what to avoid
Nearest competitor
Halyk Bank / Pay
"Reliable, proven, with you since 1923." Focus on institutional stability and trust. Traditional, conservative tone — safe but uninspiring.
VS
Kaspi Alaqan
Freedom, no limits
"The future is already in your palm." Innovative, bold, warm tone. Youthful energy + mass accessibility for every Kazakhstani.
Second competitor: Apple Pay / Google Pay Global brand, requires a smartphone and internet connection at all times. Message: "premium digital lifestyle." Our edge: Alaqan works without phone and without any network signal — an entirely different category of freedom.
What to avoid in our communication Don't copy Halyk's "reliability" narrative — that's institutional banking, not us. Never say "more convenient than a card" — Alaqan eliminates the need to carry anything at all. Avoid all technical jargon: "biometrics," "infrared scanning" belong in a white paper, not in advertising creative.
Our TOV: bold + warm + Kazakhstani Avoid: "trusted bank" framing Avoid: "technology of the future"

09 · INFLUENCERS
Micro-influencer strategy
Tiers, formats and roles within the IMC conversion funnel

Nano (1K–10K)

Neighbors, colleagues, students. Highest trust (ER 8–12%). Role: first-wave UGC "I tried it." Rewarded with product and cashback.

Micro (10K–100K)

Almaty lifestyle, food, fitness creators. ER 4–7%. Format: "a day with Alaqan." The primary working layer of the entire campaign.

Macro (100K–500K)

1–2 KOLs at launch for an awareness spike. Kazakh tech / lifestyle segment. Format: first-use unboxing and reaction.

Content formats for influencers — "Moment of delight" Unexpected real-life situation → Alaqan solves it. Examples: "wet hands after the gym — not a problem," "left my phone at home — Alaqan saves the day," "checkout in 0 seconds while the queue stares." Campaign hashtag: #EverythingInYourPalm #KaspiAlaqan
What to avoid in influencer content Direct "go buy this" ads. Competitor name-drops. Overly technical descriptions. Stock-footage shooting style — only authentic Almaty locations: real coffee shops, food courts, gyms, supermarkets. Audience immediately detects staged content.
Food creators KZ Fitness / wellness Fintech / tech KZ Almaty lifestyle Young parents (potential TA)

PHASE 1: LAUNCH · Q1 2026 — Almaty
Awareness spike: OOH + TikTok KOL + Kaspi App push campaign. Goal: 30% brand awareness among Almaty 25–38. Provocative OOH "Forget the card" at MEGA, Dostyk, City+ malls. PR launch event covered by tech and business media.
PHASE 2: GROW · Q2–Q3 2026 — national expansion
Astana, Shymkent, Karaganda, Aktobe rollout. City-specific micro-influencer activations. YouTube documentary short film. Podcast integrations. Performance retargeting on users who consumed content but haven't registered yet.
PHASE 3: LOYALTY · Q4 2026 – Q2 2027
Loyalty multiplier: enhanced cashback for Alaqan payments. Gamification inside Kaspi App. Partner promotions with Magnum, Fix Price, KFC, Starbucks KZ. Goal: TOM #1 in "contactless payment in Kazakhstan."
Integration type: Full Omni-channel with Owned Media as the hub Kaspi App is the central hub. Paid channels drive traffic into it. Earned media (UGC, word-of-mouth, PR) amplifies trust. Consistency rule: the open-palm symbol appears in every touchpoint, in every channel, without exception.
11 · CONSISTENCY
Cross-channel consistency check
How we maintain one unified brand voice across all touchpoints

Visual consistency

The open palm symbol appears in every touchpoint — terminal screen, app banner, OOH, TikTok frame, push notification icon. One recognizable sign.

Message consistency

"Everything is in your palm" runs verbatim across all channels. No variation. No dilution. From billboard to push notification — same words, same idea.

Tone consistency

Bold, warm, Kazakhstani in every format — TikTok caption, Telegram post, OOH headline, in-app copy. Never clinical, never corporate, never cold.

Kaspi App (owned) Push: "Your palm is ready — try Alaqan now." Onboarding: clean, 3-step registration, visual of open palm at each step.
TikTok / Instagram (paid + earned) Native UGC style. First 3 seconds show the "problem" (hands full / no signal). Resolution: palm on terminal. Hashtag: #EverythingInYourPalm
OOH / outdoor (offline) Headline: "Forget the card." Sub: "Pay with your palm." Visual: open hand on Kaspi-branded terminal. Red + white. Zero clutter.
Retail / terminal (point of sale) Mini-poster at every terminal: palm icon + "Alaqan accepted here." Sticker on door of participating merchants. Immediate in-store brand recognition.
